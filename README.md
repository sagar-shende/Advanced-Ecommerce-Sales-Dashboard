## 📌 Project Overview
An advanced multi-page interactive **Ecommerce Sales Dashboard** built using **Power BI**.
Analyzes 5009 orders worth **$2M in sales** and **$286K profit** across 2011–2014.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

| Page | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 🏠 Home | KPIs Overview — Orders, Sales, Profit, Margin |
| 📈 Sales Analysis | Monthly Sales & Profit, Sales by Ship Mode |
| 📦 Product Analysis | Top 10 / Bottom 10 Products, Profit by Sub-Category |
| 👥 Customers | Top 10 Customers, Sales by Segment |
| 🌍 Region & Shipping | Sales by State (Map), Profit by Region, Delivery Time |

---

## 📈 Key KPIs
- 🛒 **Total Orders:** 5,009
- 📦 **Total Quantity:** 38K
- 💰 **Total Sales:** $2M
- 💵 **Total Profit:** $286K
- 📊 **Profit Margin:** 12%
- 🚚 **Avg Delivery Days:** 3.96

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- ✅ **Technology** is the highest profit category ($0.15M)
- ✅ **Consumer segment** contributes 50.55% of total sales
- ✅ **West region** generates highest profit ($108K)
- ✅ **Standard Class** is the most used shipping mode
- ✅ **Canon imageCLA...** is the top selling product (61,600)
